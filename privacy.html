Privacy Policy

Application: SnapBlockTales (Package: com.snapblocktales.game)
Last Updated: October 22, 2026
Overview: We respect your privacy. SnapBlockTales is designed as an offline-capable casual puzzle game. We do not require account registration, and we do not collect personal identifying information like your name, address, or phone number.

1. Information Collection & Usage

SnapBlockTales does not collect any personal data directly. The game stores gameplay progress (such as unlocked levels, coin balances, best times, and star ratings) locally on your device using on-device local storage. This data is never transmitted to our private servers.

2. Third-Party Services & Advertising

The game may display third-party advertisements via Google AdMob (e.g. optional rewarded ads to earn in-game coins). These third-party services may automatically collect certain device and non-personal diagnostic information to provide personalized or contextual advertising in accordance with their respective privacy policies:

  • Google Play Services & Google AdMob: Used to display ads and handle in-game platform features. Learn more: Google Privacy Policy and Google Advertising Policies.

3. Offline Storage & Permissions

SnapBlockTales stores puzzle caching data locally on your device to ensure you can play completely offline without an active internet connection.

  • Internet Permission: Used solely to stream remote level artwork assets and request optional rewarded advertisements.
  • Vibration / Haptic: Used to provide tactile feedback during puzzle piece snapping and UI interactions.

4. Children’s Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

SnapBlockTales is intended for a general audience. We do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under the age of 13. If you believe that your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so that we can take necessary actions.

5. Data Security & Retention

Because your game progress is stored locally on your device, you have complete control over your data. You can delete all locally stored game data at any time by clearing the application storage or uninstalling the game through your device's settings.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically. Any updates will be posted directly to this URL with an updated "Last Updated" date.
